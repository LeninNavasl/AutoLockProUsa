## Brand & Style

This design system drives a modern, tech-enabled nationwide automotive locksmith and mobile vehicle security platform. The visual identity rejects the gritty, outdated tropes of legacy roadside assistance in favor of a clean, responsive, and reassuring emergency-service aesthetic. It blends the approachable precision of modern logistics interfaces with high-urgency rescue utility.

### Emotional Demeanor
- **Instant Reassurance:** Visitors are frequently stranded, locked out, or facing broken transponders. The interface delivers immediate calm through clean whitespace, crisp contrast, and unmistakable next steps.
- **Modern Reliability:** Tech-forward visual execution signals certified technicians, upfront pricing, and rapid GPS dispatching.
- **Zero Friction:** Zero walls of dense text. Scannable content structures, pill badges, and elevated action targets prioritize swift comprehension under stressful conditions.

### Aesthetic Movement
**Modern Tactile Minimalism** augmented by luminous ambient color cues. The system relies on pure white surfaces, soft canvas backdrops (`#F8FAFC`), deliberate colored icon wells, micro-borders (`#E2E8F0`), and prominent call-to-action buttons. High-contrast typography pairs with friendly curves (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`) to project technical capability without clinical coldness.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative technical competence with high-visibility urgency cues.

### Primary Palette
- **Electric Blue (`#2563EB`):** Represents certified engineering, dependable dispatch networks, and digital precision. Used for primary navigation states, verified technician badges, selection states, and brand anchors.
- **Emergency Coral Orange (`#F97316`):** The primary conversion trigger. Dedicated strictly to high-priority emergency actions, notably direct phone dispatch links (`tel:18005550199`) and urgent mobile help buttons.
- **Soft Emerald (`#10B981`):** Applied as a functional status indicator—live technician availability, verified pricing, success confirmations, and active 24/7 dispatch indicators.
- **Luminous Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Accent tone paired with Electric Blue in subtle ambient radial gradients and directional decorative strokes.

### Neutrals & Background Surfaces
- **Deep Slate (`#0F172A`):** The grounding typography tone for headers and primary data points, ensuring WCAG AAA legibility in outdoor daylight glare.
- **Muted Slate (`#64748B`):** Secondary tone for structural body copy, specifications, and supplementary captions.
- **Border Slate (`#E2E8F0` / `#F1F5F9`):** Thin containment borders that frame white content cards on soft canvas backgrounds.
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft, anti-glare background providing visual separation from stark white (`#FFFFFF`) component cards.

## Typography

The typography system is unified under **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its geometric underpinnings convey technical precision, while sculpted humanist curves avoid harsh utilitarian sterility.

### Hierarchy & Legibility Rules
- **No Dense Paragraphs:** Limit continuous descriptive copy to a maximum of 3 lines (under 45 characters per line on mobile). Chunk information into value cards, process steppers, and scannable checklist rows.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Service arrival times (e.g., "15–25 MIN"), phone numbers, and pricing starts are rendered in bold or extra-bold weights to anchor visual hierarchy.
- **All-Caps Usage:** Confined solely to `label-sm` badges and status pills (e.g., "MOBILE DISPATCH", "24/7 ON CALL") with generous letter spacing (`0.04em`) to maintain legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured, content-constrained 12-column grid system on desktop (max width `1280px`), adapting to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints
- **Mobile:** `< 640px` (single-column card stacks, sticky bottom call bar, compact padding).
- **Tablet:** `640px – 1024px` (2-column grids for service offerings, condensed inline metrics).
- **Desktop:** `> 1024px` (balanced multi-column service matrices, side-by-side callout zones).

### Density Rhythm
- **Breathing Space:** Section padding adheres to `space-xl` multiplied by factors of 2 (`5rem` / `80px`) on desktop to maintain clean separation between offerings.
- **Interactive Cluster Proximity:** High-priority items (e.g., an icon, vehicle make badge, and immediate call link) stay tightly grouped using `space-sm` to `space-md` gaps to retain immediate contextual meaning.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses an ambient, multi-layered elevation model rather than muddy drop shadows, ensuring cards float crisp and cleanly over the `#F8FAFC` base canvas.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background surface (`#F8FAFC`). May feature faint radial atmospheric glows (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.04)` to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.03)`).
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Service Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered / Active Cards):** Elevated surface with an electric or coral-tinted ambient halo: `0 12px 30px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Micro-shift `-2px` on the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Fixed Utility & Conversion Elements):** Floating sticky mobile dispatch bar, header surface, and emergency dialogs: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`, supported by backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) when semi-transparent.

## Shapes

The design uses a generous curved language (`roundedness: 2`) with specialized treatments:

### Shape Roles
- **Primary Content Cards:** `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) to `rounded-3xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for hero containers, testimonial blocks, and service profiles.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons & Inputs):** Standardized on `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) for structured fields, or fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) for high-conversion phone CTAs and emergency dispatch buttons.
- **Icon Containers:** Soft squircle pods (`rounded-2xl`) with solid or subtle 10% opacity color backings.
- **Status Badges & Chips:** Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Header (Minimal & Focused)
- **Structure:** Left-aligned brand lockup featuring a geometric key/shield icon mark in Electric Blue + Deep Slate wordmark "AutoLock Pro USA". Centered or right-aligned minimal navigation: `[ Home | Services | Blog | Contact ]`.
- **Exclusions:** Strictly no user login, no search input, no account avatars, and no complex mega-menus.
- **Desktop Right Anchor:** High-contrast pill CTA button styled in Coral Orange (`#F97316`) pointing directly to `tel:18005550199` with an integrated phone icon.

### Emergency Call CTAs
- **Primary Style:** Pill-shaped, Coral Orange (`#F97316`) solid background with pure white typography (`label-lg`), equipped with a pulsing phone receiver icon.
- **Behavior:** Explicit hyperlink binding to `tel:18005550199`.
- **Mobile Persistent Bar:** A fixed bottom anchor bar on screens `< 640px` that features the phone CTA spanning full width, accompanied by an Emerald "Live Dispatcher Available" indicator.

### Service & Benefit Cards
- **Construction:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, `rounded-2xl` or `rounded-3xl`, bordered with `#E2E8F0`.
- **Interior Layout:** Top-left 48x48px icon container filled with an accent tint (`#EFF6FF` for blue icons, `#FFF7ED` for orange icons), followed by a bold headline (`headline-sm`), an ultra-concise 2-line explanation, and an inline pill tag (e.g., "15-Min Arrival", "Key Fob Programming").

### Pill Badges & Chips
- **Design:** `rounded-full`, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, `label-sm` font weight.
- **Status Variants:**
  - *Emergency/Active:* Pale emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), bold emerald text (`#059669`), paired with a pulsing 6px green live status dot.
  - *Service Spec:* Slate background (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#475569`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Visuals:** Height 48px, `rounded-xl`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Focus ring: 3px semi-transparent electric blue (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`), border changes to `#2563EB`.
- **Context:** Optimized for mobile thumb targets when entering vehicle year/make/model or ZIP code.

### Specialized Component: Live Dispatch Radar
- A lightweight UI widget displaying approximate nationwide mobile locksmith coverage with a live pulsing green indicator: "Technicians On Call in [Region/Metro Area] • Typical ETA 15–30m".
